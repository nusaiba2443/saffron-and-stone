A table for everyone

Familiar comfort. A world of flavour.

Saffron & Stone is a contemporary family restaurant concept shaped around one simple idea: a meal should bring people together. Our menu borrows inspiration from kitchens across the world, while keeping the experience relaxed, generous and easy to enjoy.

We care about the details — a thoughtful plate, a welcoming room, a little extra time for conversation. Whether it's a weekday lunch or a celebration with the whole family, we want you to feel like you belong here.
